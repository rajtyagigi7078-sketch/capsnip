Quick answers and direct support for your local caption studio.
Getting started
How do I make my first captions?

Import a video, choose a caption style, and select Generate captions. When transcription finishes, edit any transcript line directly. Select another style and press Apply changes to preview it.

Can I use Hinglish?

Yes. Choose Hindi + English (Hinglish) in the editor. Speech recognition runs locally using the detected CPU/GPU setup.

Where are my projects and exports stored?

Projects are saved as local Capsnip files. MP4 exports go to Videos / Capsnip Exports, or the folder you choose in Settings.

Local processing

Your source video and transcript stay on this computer. Capsnip transcribes locally and exports H.264 MP4 files.
